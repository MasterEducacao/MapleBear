Irregular Verbs · Verbos Irregulares

Irregular verbs are verbs that do not follow the regular rule when we change them to the past. Instead of adding -ed, they change in a completely different way.

Regular verbs → add -ed in the past. Irregular verbs → change in a different way (they do not follow one rule).
Verbos regulares adicionam -ed no passado. Verbos irregulares mudam de forma diferente — cada um à sua maneira.
Regular Verbs — add -ed:
play → played walk → walked jump → jumped
Irregular Verbs — change differently:
go → went eat → ate see → saw

Past Perfect · Passado Perfeito

We use HAD + past participle to talk about something that happened before another action in the past. This is called the Past Perfect.

Structure: Subject + HAD + past participle — use HAD + past participle for the FIRST action in the past.
Estrutura: Sujeito + HAD + particípio passado — usamos para a ação que aconteceu PRIMEIRO no passado.
Examples · Exemplos:

E.g 1: I had eaten before school. (Eu tinha comido antes da escola.)

E.g 2: She had finished her homework before dinner. (Ela tinha terminado o dever antes do jantar.)

E.g 3: I had eaten before I went to school.

→ What happened first? I had eaten ✅  |  What happened second? I went to school

When do we use "had + past participle"?
We use it when two things happened in the past, and we want to show which one happened first.

More examples · Mais exemplos:

E.g 4: She had finished the book before the test.

E.g 5: We had studied before the exam.

E.g 6: He had gone home before it rained.

📖 Vocabulário · Key Words
Past Perfect
Passado perfeito: had + particípio. / Had + past participle tense.
HAD
Auxiliar do Past Perfect (passado de "have"). / Auxiliary verb for past perfect.
Past participle
Terceira coluna dos verbos. Ex: eaten, done, gone.
Before
Antes — conecta as duas ações no passado. / Connects the two past actions.
First action
A ação que aconteceu primeiro usa HAD. / The action that happened first uses HAD.

Present Continuous · Presente Contínuo

The Present Continuous is used to talk about things happening now, at this moment. It describes actions currently in progress.

Structure: am / is / are + verb + ing — use it for actions happening NOW or at this moment.
Estrutura: am/is/are + verbo + ing — usado para ações que estão acontecendo AGORA ou neste momento.
Subject · SujeitoVerb "to be"Example
IamI am reading a book.
He / She / ItisShe is writing a paragraph.
You / We / TheyareThey are working together.

We use the Present Continuous for:

✅ Actions happening now · Ações acontecendo agora

✅ Actions happening at the moment · Ações acontecendo neste momento

✅ Temporary actions · Ações temporárias


E.g 4: I am doing my homework now.

E.g 5: She is reading a novel.

E.g 6: We are helping our classmates.

Presente contínuo: am/is/are + verb+ing.

-ing form

Temporário — algo que não é permanente.

The Writing Process · O Processo de Escrita

The Writing Process is the steps we follow to write a good text. Good writers do not write everything at once — they write step by step to make their writing better.

The Writing Process has 5 steps: Prewriting → Drafting → Revising → Editing → Publishing.
O processo de escrita tem 5 etapas: Pré-escrita → Rascunho → Revisão → Edição → Publicação.
1

Prewriting · Pré-escrita (Think and Plan)

This is when you think about your ideas and plan your writing. Brainstorm ideas and organize your thoughts.

2

Drafting · Rascunho (Write)

This is when you write your first text (first draft). Get your ideas down — don't worry about mistakes yet.

3

Revising · Revisão (Make it Better)

This is when you improve your writing. Add and change ideas to make it better.

4

Editing · Edição (Fix Mistakes)

This is when you fix the mistakes — check spelling, fix punctuation, correct grammar.

5

Publishing · Publicação (Final Copy)

This is when you write the final copy and share your work with others.

📖 Vocabulário · Key Words
Prewriting
Planejar e organizar ideias antes de escrever.
Drafting
Escrever o primeiro rascunho do texto.
Revising
Melhorar o conteúdo do texto.
Editing
Corrigir erros de ortografia, pontuação e gramática.
Publishing
Escrever a versão final e compartilhar.
Draft
Rascunho — versão inicial, não final.

Linking Verbs · Verbos de Ligação

Linking verbs are verbs that connect the subject to a description or state. They do not show action — they describe what the subject is or feels like.

Linking verbs: am, is, are, was, were, seem, become — they connect the subject to a description, feeling, or state.
Verbos de ligação: am, is, are, was, were, seem, become — conectam o sujeito a uma descrição, sentimento ou estado.
Linking Verbs — Main Examples:
am · is · are was · were seem · become
Examples with characters · Exemplos com os personagens:

👦 Peter is frustrated. (Peter está frustrado.)

😄 Fudge is funny. (Fudge é engraçado.)

👨‍👩‍👦 His parents are patient. (Seus pais são pacientes.)

📖 The story seems interesting. (A história parece interessante.)

⭐ Peter becomes more patient. (Peter se torna mais paciente.)

We use linking verbs to describe characters, feelings, and events from stories — for example from Tales of a Fourth Grade Nothing.

Verbs & Verbals · Verbos e Verbais

Verbs are action words. They show what someone or something does.

Verb = action. Verbal = a word that comes from a verb but acts as a NOUN (not an action) in the sentence.
Verbo = ação. Verbal = palavra derivada de um verbo que funciona como SUBSTANTIVO (não ação) na frase.
Verbs · Verbos (action = ação):

• I run every day.  • She reads a book.  • They play soccer.  • He writes a story.

Verb = action 🏃

Verbals are words that come from verbs, but they do not work as verbs in the sentence — they work as nouns (subjects or objects).

Verbals · Verbais (noun = substantivo):

• Reading is fun.  • Playing soccer is exciting.

• Writing is important.  • Drawing is my favorite activity.

• Peter likes to help his mom.

In these sentences, reading, playing, writing, drawing are activities — so they are nouns, not actions.

📖 Vocabulário · Key Words
Verb
Palavra de ação. Ex: run, jump, write. / Action word.
Verbal
Derivado de verbo, funciona como substantivo. / Verb-derived noun.
Gerund (-ing)
Verbal: verbo + -ing como substantivo. Ex: Reading is fun.
Infinitive (to+verb)
Verbal: to + verbo. Ex: Peter likes to help.
Noun
Substantivo — pode ser sujeito ou objeto. / Subject or object word.

Adjectives & Personality Traits

Adjectives are words that describe nouns (people, places, animals, or things). They tell us what something is like.

Adjectives describe what you CAN SEE — appearance, size, color, feelings, things. (tall, big, red, beautiful)
Adjetivos descrevem o que você PODE VER — aparência, tamanho, cor, sentimentos e coisas. (alto, grande, vermelho, bonito)
Adjectives can describe · Adjetivos podem descrever:
👁️ appearance 📏 size 🎨 color 💛 feelings 🪑 things

E.g 1: The boy is tall.   E.g 2: The car is fast.   E.g 3: The flower is beautiful.   E.g 4: She is happy.

Personality Traits are words that describe how a person behaves or how a person is inside. They describe a person's character, not appearance.

Personality Traits describe how a person BEHAVES — character and behavior. (kind, honest, helpful, funny)
Traços de personalidade descrevem como uma pessoa SE COMPORTA — caráter e comportamento. (gentil, honesto, prestativo, engraçado)
Personality Traits examples:

E.g 5: She is kind.   E.g 6: He is very helpful.   E.g 7: My friend is funny.   E.g 8: The student is responsible.

ELA 9 · Seção 9 of 10
Comparatives & Superlatives

Comparatives are used to compare two things. We usually add -er + than or use more + adjective + than.

Comparative = compare 2 things → add -er + than (shorter adjectives) OR more + adjective + than (longer adjectives).
Comparativo = comparar 2 coisas → adicionar -er + than (adjetivos curtos) OU more + adjetivo + than (adjetivos longos).
Comparative examples:

E.g 1: Peter is taller than Fudge.   E.g 2: This book is longer than that book.

E.g 3: This story is more interesting than the other story.   E.g 4: Math is easier than science.

Superlatives are used to compare three or more things. We usually add -est or use the most + adjective.

Superlative = compare 3 or more things → add the -est (shorter adjectives) OR the most + adjective (longer adjectives).
Superlativo = comparar 3 ou mais coisas → adicionar the -est (adjetivos curtos) OU the most + adjetivo (adjetivos longos).
Superlative examples:

E.g 5: Peter is the tallest boy in the class.   E.g 6: This is the longest chapter.

E.g 7: This is the most interesting book.   E.g 8: That was the easiest test.

Adverbs of Manner · Advérbios de Modo

Adverbs of manner tell us how an action happens. They describe how someone does something. They usually come after the verb.

Adverbs of manner describe HOW an action happens. We usually add -ly to an adjective to form the adverb. (quick → quickly)
Advérbios de modo descrevem COMO uma ação acontece. Geralmente adicionamos -ly ao adjetivo. (rápido → rapidamente)
Examples · Exemplos (add -ly):
• He runs quickly.• She speaks softly. • They walk slowly.• The student writes neatly. • The teacher speaks clearly.• He works carefully. • She sings beautifully.• The baby sleeps quietly.

⚠️ Exceptions · Exceções: Some adverbs do NOT add -ly — the adjective and adverb have the same form:
