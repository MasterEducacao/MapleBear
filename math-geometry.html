What is a Triangle? O que é um Triângulo?

A triangletriângulo is a polygonpolígono with exactly 3 sideslados and 3 anglesângulos. Each corner where two sides meet is called a vertexvértice (plural: verticesvértices).

The sum of all interior angles of any triangle always equals 180°.
A soma de todos os ângulos interiores de qualquer triângulo é sempre igual a 180°.

Triangles are one of the most fundamental shapes in geometrygeometria. They appear everywhere in architecture, engineering, and nature because the triangle is the only rigidrígido polygon — it cannot be deformed without changing the length of a side.

Classifying by Angles Por Ângulos

We can classify triangles by looking at their anglesângulos. There are three types based on the size of the largest angle.

Right Triangle
Triângulo Retângulo
1 angle = 90°
Acute Triangle
Triângulo Acutângulo
All angles < 90°
Obtuse Triangle
Triângulo Obtusângulo
1 angle > 90°
A right triangle has one angle of exactly 90°, marked with a small square symbol (□).
Um triângulo retângulo tem um ângulo de exatamente 90°, marcado com um símbolo de quadrado pequeno (□).
💡 The side opposite the right angle in a right triangle is called the hypotenuse (hipotenusa) — it is always the longest side.

To identify triangle types, mathematicians use a protractortransferidor to measure anglesângulos and a rulerrégua to measure sideslados.

Acute · Agudo

Obtuse · Obtuso
Greater than 90°. Maior que 90°.
Hypotenuse · Hipotenusa
Longest side of a right triangle.
Protractor · Transferidor
Tool used to measure angles.
Degrees (°) · Graus
Unit used to measure angles.

Section 3 · Seção 3
Classifying by Sides Por Lados

We can also classify triangles by the lengthcomprimento of their sideslados. Small tick marks (✓) on sides show which sides are equal (iguais).

Equilateral
Equilátero
3 equal sides · 60° each
Isosceles
Isósceles
2 equal sides
Scalene
Escaleno
No equal sides
An equilateral triangle has all sides equal and all angles equal to 60°. An isosceles triangle has exactly two equal sides. A scalene triangle has all sides different.
Um triângulo equilátero tem todos os lados iguais. Um triângulo isósceles tem dois lados iguais. Um triângulo escaleno tem todos os lados diferentes.
💡 In an isosceles triangle, the two base anglesângulos da base (opposite the equal sides) are also equal.

⚡ Combining Both Classifications

These two systems work together. A triangle can be described by both its angle type and its side type:

Right + Scalene
One 90° angle + all sides different.
Retângulo + Escaleno
Acute + Isosceles
All angles < 90° + two equal sides.
Acutângulo + Isósceles
Equilateral → always Acute
60° + 60° + 60° = always < 90°.
Equilátero → sempre Acutângulo
