Irregular Verbs · Verbos Irregulares

Irregular verbs are verbs that do NOT follow the regular rule when changed to the past. Regular verbs simply add -ed; irregular verbs change in their own unique way.

Regular verbs → add -ed in the past. Irregular verbs → change in a different way (they do not follow one rule).
Verbos regulares adicionam -ed no passado. Verbos irregulares mudam de forma diferente — cada um à sua maneira.
✅ Regular Verbs (add -ed)play → played
walk → walked
jump → jumped
⚡ Irregular Verbs (own rule)go → went
eat → ate
see → saw
🧠
Dica de Memória

Alguns verbos irregulares são "unchanging" — ficam iguais nas 3 formas: cut→cut→cut, put→put→put, cost→cost→cost.

Past Perfect · Passado Perfeito

We use HAD + past participle to talk about something that happened BEFORE another action in the past.

Structure: Subject + HAD + past participle — use HAD + past participle for the FIRST action in the past.
Estrutura: Sujeito + HAD + particípio passado. Em português: Pretérito Mais-que-perfeito — "Eu já tinha comido quando ela chegou."
HAD + participle (FIRST)
→
Simple Past (SECOND)
→
NOW
💡
Como Lembrar

O que veio PRIMEIRO usa HAD! "I had eaten before I went to school." → comer veio antes de ir.

Examples · Exemplos:

I had eaten before I went to school.

She had finished her homework before dinner.

We had studied all night before the exam.

He had gone home before it rained.

ELA 3 · Seção 3 of 10
Present Continuous · Presente Contínuo

The Present Continuous is used to talk about things happening NOW, at this exact moment.

Structure: am / is / are + verb + -ING — use for actions happening NOW.
Estrutura: am/is/are + verbo + ing. Em português: gerúndio — "Eu estou lendo", "Ela está escrevendo".
SubjectTo BeExample
IamI am reading a book.
He / She / ItisShe is writing a paragraph.
You / We / TheyareThey are playing soccer.
🧠
Regra CVC

CVC = Consoante + Vogal + Consoante. Ex: r-u-n. Se termina nesse padrão → dobra a última letra! run→running, sit→sitting. Verbo com -e: remove o -e → write→writing.

The Writing Process · O Processo de Escrita

The Writing Process is the 5 steps we follow to write a good text. Good writers do NOT write everything at once — they write step by step.

The Writing Process has 5 steps: Prewriting → Drafting → Revising → Editing → Publishing.
É como construir uma casa: você planeja → levanta a estrutura → melhora → conserta erros → entrega!
1

Prewriting — Think & Plan

Brainstorm ideas, organize thoughts, use mind maps or lists.

💡 Antes de escrever, anote 3 ideias sobre o tema!
2

Drafting — Write

Write your first draft. Get ideas down. Don't worry about mistakes!

💡 Foque em colocar as ideias no papel. Erros serão corrigidos depois.
3

Revising — Make it Better

Improve ideas, add details, make text clearer and more interesting.

💡 Pergunte-se: "Faz sentido? Posso adicionar mais detalhes?"
4

Editing — Fix Mistakes

Check spelling, punctuation, and grammar errors.

💡 Leia devagar e verifique cada frase!
5

Publishing — Final Copy

Write the final, clean copy and share your work with others.

💡 O texto final deve estar organizado e pronto para ser lido!
🔑
Revising vs Editing

Revising = melhorar as IDEIAS e o conteúdo.
Editing = corrigir GRAMÁTICA, ORTOGRAFIA e PONTUAÇÃO.

ELA 5 · Seção 5 of 10
Linking Verbs · Verbos de Ligação

Linking verbs do NOT show action. They CONNECT the subject to more information — usually an adjective or a noun describing a state or feeling.

Linking verbs: am, is, are, was, were, seem, become, feel, look, smell, taste, sound — they connect the subject to a description.
Verbos de ligação conectam o sujeito a uma descrição, sentimento ou estado — não mostram ação física.
⚡ Action VerbShe RUNS every day.
(shows action)
🔗 Linking VerbShe IS happy.
(connects to adjective)
🔗 Common Linking Verbs
am / is / arewas / wereseem becomefeellook smelltastesound
Examples with characters:

👦 Peter is frustrated because Fudge always causes problems.

👶 Fudge is funny but very naughty.

🌸 The flowers look beautiful.

🍲 The soup smells wonderful.

⭐ Peter becomes more patient at the end of the story.

Verbs & Verbals · Verbos e Verbais

Verbs are action words — they show what someone does. Verbals come from verbs but work as nouns in the sentence (subjects or objects).

Verb = action. Verbal = a word from a verb that acts as a NOUN (subject or object) in the sentence.
Verbo = ação. Verbal = palavra derivada de um verbo que funciona como SUBSTANTIVO (sujeito ou objeto) na frase.
⚡ VERB — action wordI RUN every day.
She READS a book.
shows what someone DOES
📌 VERBAL — acts as nounREADING is fun.
PLAYING is exciting.
names an ACTIVITY (subject)
TipoEstruturaExemploFunção
Gerundverb + -ingReading is fun.Subject (noun)
Infinitiveto + verbPeter likes to help.Noun / object

Comparatives & Superlatives
📊 Comparative — Compara 2 coisasAdjective + -er + than
or more + adjective + than
Peter is taller than Fudge.
🏆 Superlative — Compara 3+ coisasthe + adjective + -est
or the most + adjective
Peter is the tallest in the class.
Comparative = compare 2 things → add -er + than OR more + adjective + than. Superlative = compare 3+ things → the -est OR the most.
Comparativo = 2 coisas. Superlativo = 3 ou mais coisas.
AdjectiveComparativeSuperlativeRegra
talltallerthe tallest+er / +est

bigbiggerthe biggestCVC → dobra

Irregulares Importantes

bad → worse → the worst

Adverbs of Manner · Advérbios de Modo

Adverbs of manner tell us HOW an action happens. They usually come after the verb. Most are formed by adding -LY to an adjective.

Adverbs of manner describe HOW an action happens. Add -ly to the adjective: quick → quickly, soft → softly.
Advérbios de modo descrevem COMO uma ação acontece. Em português terminam em -mente: quickly = rapidamente.
RegraExemplo
Most: add -lyquick → quickly, soft → softly
Ends in -y: → -ilyhappy → happily, easy → easily
Ends in -le: → -lygentle → gently
⚠️ good → well (irregular)She sings well.
⚠️ fast → fast (no change)He runs fast.
⚠️ hard → hard (no change)She works hard.
